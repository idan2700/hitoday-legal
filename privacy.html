Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Hitoday (“we”, “the app”) is a daily music games app. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account identifiers. We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication. You get a unique user ID without creating an email/password account.
  • Profile. Optional display name and a friend code used to connect with friends.
  • Gameplay data. Scores, game progress, streaks-related fields, and leaderboard rankings (daily and all-time).
  • Friends list. User IDs of people you add via friend codes (and reciprocal links).
  • Technical data. Standard Firebase/device diagnostics may include app version and crash-related information if enabled by the platform.

We do not require your real name, email, phone number, or payment information to play.

How we use information

  • To run daily games and sync progress across devices for your anonymous account
  • To show global and friends leaderboards
  • To let you add friends and compete with them
  • To improve stability and fix bugs

Music and third-party services

  • Deezer — short audio previews and track metadata for games
  • YouTube Data API — view counts used in View Battle (when configured)
  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, and cloud functions

These services process data under their own policies when you use features that call them.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to operate the app (e.g. Google Firebase) and when required by law.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account). This removes your profile, progress, friend links, and friend code, and signs you out. A new anonymous guest account may be created if you keep using the app.

Children

Hitoday is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@hitoday.app

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
