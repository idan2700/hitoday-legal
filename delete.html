Request deletion of your data

For SongQuest (SongQuest: Music daily games), also known previously as Hitoday.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

How to delete your data (recommended)

  1. Open the SongQuest app on your device.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account data associated with the app.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Auth account for SongQuest
  • Profile (display name, friend code)
  • Game progress and scores
  • Friends list / friend links related to your account
  • Leaderboard entries tied to your account

What is kept

  • We do not keep a backup of your personal profile after deletion for continued use of the app.
  • Aggregated or anonymized statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.
  • Limited records may be retained only if required by law or to prevent abuse (for example, fraud prevention), for as long as legally necessary.
  • Third-party services we use (such as Google Firebase, Deezer, or YouTube when used) may process technical data under their own policies for a limited retention period.

After deletion

Deletion cannot be undone. If you open SongQuest again, a new anonymous guest account may be created. That new account will not restore your old scores or friends.

Request by email (if you cannot use the app)

Email support@hitoday.app with the subject “SongQuest data deletion”. Include your in-app display name and friend code if you have them, so we can locate your account. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.
